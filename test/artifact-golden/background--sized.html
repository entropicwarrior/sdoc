TWO-VALUE SIZE

A size the keywords cannot say

background-size: auto 100% — full height, natural width, aspect kept.

Why a pair

No fit keyword says this. cover scales until both edges are covered and crops the rest; contain fits the whole picture inside the slide, which for a tall image means fitting it by width.

What it does

A pair sizes the image's own box, so the picture is exactly that size and background-position places it as a whole. Either value may be auto to follow the aspect ratio.

With the rest

background-scale: multiplies the pair, so auto 100% at 0.5 is half-height. Flips and fades behave exactly as they do with a keyword.
