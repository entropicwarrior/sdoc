A linear fade across the slide

linear angle=90 from=25% to=85% — full strength to a quarter across, gone by 85%.

angle

The direction the falloff runs, in degrees. 90 is left to right; 180 is top to bottom.

from and to

Where the falloff starts and finishes along that direction. The gap between them is the softness of the edge.

min and max

The alpha at each end. Defaults are 0 and 1, so the image runs from solid to invisible.
