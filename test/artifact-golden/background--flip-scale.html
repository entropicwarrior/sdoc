FLIP AND SCALE

Flip and scale

background-flip: horizontal with background-scale: 0.6, placed right center.

The picture stays put

A flip does not move the image. Placed against the right edge and flipped, it stays on the right and faces the other way. background-position always says where the image ends up.

The fade stays put too

The fade is applied to the slide rather than to the picture, so flipping the image never drags the protected area away from the words it was protecting.

Scale shares the anchor

background-scale: 0.6 shrinks about the same point, so an image pinned to an edge stays pinned instead of drifting to the middle.
